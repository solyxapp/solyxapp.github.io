Help with Solyx

Support

We’re here to help.

For help with Solyx, email support@solyxapp.com. Include a short description of what happened, your iPhone or iPad model, and your iOS or iPadOS version. Please do not send passwords or payment information.

Contact support

Purchases and subscriptions

Use Restore Purchases in Solyx if a previous subscription or Lifetime purchase is not recognized. You can manage or cancel a subscription from your Apple Account subscription settings. Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically until canceled.

Choose a monthly or yearly subscription, or Lifetime access for a one-time purchase of $79.99 USD. The app shows prices in your local currency before purchase. Lifetime is a non-consumable purchase and does not renew. You do not need a subscription with Lifetime.

Reminders

If reminders are not arriving, open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, select Notifications, choose Solyx, and confirm that notifications are allowed. You can edit each habit’s reminders from Solyx.

Your data

Solyx stores habit and Galaxy data locally. You can export it from Settings or use Delete Galaxy for a full in-app reset.

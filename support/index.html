Support

Help with Solyx

For help with Solyx, email solyxapp@proton.me. Include a short description of what happened, your iPhone model, and your iOS version. Please do not send passwords or payment information.

Contact support

Premium purchases

Use Restore Purchases on the Solyx Premium screen if a previous subscription is not recognized. You can manage or cancel a subscription from your Apple Account subscription settings.

Reminders

If reminders are not arriving, open iPhone Settings, select Notifications, choose Solyx, and confirm that notifications are allowed. You can edit each habit's reminder from Solyx.

Your data

Solyx stores habit and Galaxy data locally. You can export it from Settings or use Delete Galaxy for a full in-app reset.

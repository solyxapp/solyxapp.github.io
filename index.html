How do habits become a galaxy?

Each habit receives a planet. Logging completions builds stars, which form constellations over time. Completions also bring you closer to discovering new planets.

How much does Solyx cost?

Choose a monthly or yearly subscription, or Lifetime access with a one-time purchase of $79.99 USD. Solyx Monthly is $6.99 USD a month and Solyx Yearly is $29.99 USD a year. The app shows prices in your local currency and the full terms before you purchase.

Eligible new subscribers can try the yearly plan free for 7 days. Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically until canceled. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and does not renew. You do not need a subscription with Lifetime.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can manage or cancel a subscription from your Apple Account subscription settings, and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Lifetime never renews, so there is nothing to cancel.

Do I need an account?

No. Your habits and galaxy are stored on your device. Apple handles purchases, and RevenueCat manages purchase access and limited purchase analytics. Read our privacy policy for the details.

Will it work on my device?

Solyx is built for iPhone running iOS 17 or later. An Android version has not been announced.

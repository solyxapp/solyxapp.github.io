Frequently asked questions

A few things to know.

Where can I download Solyx?

Solyx is available now on the App Store for iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.

Will Solyx be available on Android?

Solyx is available for iPhone and iPad. There is no announced Android release yet.

Does Solyx require an account?

No. Your habits and galaxy are stored on your device. Apple handles purchases, and RevenueCat manages subscription access.

Is Solyx free?

Solyx requires a monthly or yearly subscription. Eligible new subscribers can try the yearly plan free for 7 days. Prices and renewal terms are shown before subscribing.
